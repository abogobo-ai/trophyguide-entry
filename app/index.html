    The road to platinum — six chapters, one save per chapter

    Play the story in order, start the missable meditation in Chapter 1, and save at the start of every chapter. The cleanup in Stage 6 is where the platinum is won.

    Save-slot planner — tick each save as you make it

      No mission replay. No New Game+. Never play with cheats on your trophy save. One clean save before the final mission is your safety net.

        Missables — one trophy, one rule

        The save rules that protect you

        1. Save at the start of every chapter — six manual saves, one per chapter, plus one clean save before the final mission.
        2. No mission replay. A missed moment cannot be replayed; your chapter save is the only way back.
        3. No New Game+. There is no second run that carries progress over — one save, one road.
        4. No cheats on the trophy save. Cheats can disable trophies; keep a separate fun save for chaos.
        5. Do the first meditation the morning after Down to Earth — Lucia on Jason's balcony, before Chapter 1 ends.

        Meditation spots — 0 / 18

        All 18 spots stay open for the whole game except the first one. Use the counter on the trophy to track them.

        • Time estimates are our own judgment from the descriptions and the community's early playthroughs — your mileage will vary by a few hours either way.
